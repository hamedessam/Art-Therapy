Who we are

More Than a Workshop

Art Therapy is a room where your hands get to think for a while.

We're a creative space for anyone who wants to express themselves, chase a stray idea, and try a material they've never touched before — paint, clay, thread, paper, colour. No experience required, no "talent" prerequisite. Just time set aside to make something, breathe a little slower, and leave lighter than you arrived.

Every session is guided by working artists who care more about your process than your result — because the quiet restoration happens in the making, not the finished piece.
